## Code of conduct {#code-of-conduct}

Everyone in this course is responsible for making it a place where people can
show unfinished work, play each other's games honestly, and give and take
criticism without it becoming personal. Game design only works if playtesters
tell you the truth and designers can hear it.

The ANU [Code of Conduct](https://www.anu.edu.au/students/program-administration/program-management/anu-code-of-conduct-for-students)
applies to all students.

## Academic integrity {#integrity}

All work you submit must be your own, or your team's, with any use of other
people's assets, code or ideas clearly attributed. See the ANU
[academic integrity](https://www.anu.edu.au/students/academic-skills/academic-integrity)
pages.
