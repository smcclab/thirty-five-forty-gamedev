The weekly workshops are the **most important learning activity** in this
course. This is where you build, where you playtest with other people in the
studio, and where you find out whether your game is doing what you think it
is doing.

## What to do

*Before* the workshop, read that week's lecture material and bring something
playable — a paper prototype, a sketch, a build, whatever the week calls for.
Each week's page lists:

- the design activity for the week
- what to bring or prepare
- readings and videos
