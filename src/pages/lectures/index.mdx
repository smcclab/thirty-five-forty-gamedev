Lectures cover the game design theory you apply in your workshops and in your
own game. In 2024 the theory was delivered as short per-topic decks:

| Week | Topics |
| ---- | ------ |
| 1 | Play and games; formal elements |
| 2 | The playcentric design process; idea generation; prototyping |
| 3 | Engaging the player; designing to engage; flow, needs and motivation |
| 4 | Systems; mechanics; balance |
| 5 | Interface design; level design |
| 7 | Agile game development |
| 8 | Learning and training; challenge, difficulty and pacing |
| 9 | The playtesting process; conducting playtesting |

The set texts are Tracy Fullerton, *Game Design Workshop*, and Jesse Schell,
*The Art of Game Design*; the lectures cite both throughout.
