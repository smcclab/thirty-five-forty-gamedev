## What is Game Development?

COMP3540/6540 is a studio course in making digital games. You will learn what
makes play work --- the formal elements of games, the shape of player
experience --- and then put that to work by generating ideas, building
prototypes, playtesting them with real players, and producing a finished game
in a team.

The course is built around the *playcentric* design process: you design for the
experience the player has, and you find out what that experience actually is by
putting the game in front of people early and often.

## What happens in the course?

Each week you attend a lecture covering game design theory, and a workshop
where you work on your game with your team and playtest with the rest of the
studio. Across the semester you keep a design diary, run design experiments,
write a proposal for your game, and build and ship it.

## Getting started

1. [Enrol in the course](https://cecc.anu.edu.au/students/continuing/enrolling/) and sign up for a [workshop](/workshops/) on MyTimetable.
2. Make sure you have a [laptop](/policies/#laptop) that can run a game engine, and commit to attending all lectures and workshops.
3. Read through the [tools](/resources/tools/) page and install what you need.
4. Browse the [reading list](/resources/reading/) for a head start on game design theory.

**Show up in week 1 ready to make games.**
