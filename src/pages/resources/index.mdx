What you need to get set up, what to work through, and what to read.

[Tools](/resources/tools/) covers the software: the engine, the accounts you
need and where your work gets published. The
[Unity pathway](/resources/unity-pathway/) sets out the self-paced tutorial
sequence that runs through the first half of semester and explains how each
unit feeds the prototype assessments. [Reading](/resources/reading/) maps the
set texts to each theory topic and lists the other works the lectures draw on.
[Past student games](/resources/showcase/) shows what teams have built here
before, and the [FAQ](/resources/faq/) answers the questions that come up
before and during week 1.
