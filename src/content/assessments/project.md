## Outline

- **Due date:** 2024 was 5pm Monday of the exam period (28 October); peer assessment a week later
- **Mark weighting:** 35% — game 35%, coversheet 0%, peer assessment 0%
- **Collaboration:** group
- **Submission:** game to Unity Play, coversheet via Wattle, peer assessment via MS Forms

## Description

In a small team, build and publish a playable game.

Restrictions: single-player, playable on a computer with keyboard and mouse,
published as a WebGL build to Unity Play, and **playable in 15 minutes** —
that is how long your markers will play it, whether it is shorter or longer.

Your game is marked by your peers through playtesting, on functionality,
learning, interest, controls, interface, feedback, enjoyment, originality,
pacing and usability.

### Assets

Free assets from the Unity Asset Store, the Unity Course Library and approved
sources may be used for **graphics, sound and animation only** — not for code
or functionality. Paid assets are not permitted. Everything you use must be
declared on the coversheet.

### Coversheet and peer assessment

Both are worth 0% but both are mandatory: an individual mark of 0 is given for
the game if either is missing or late.
