## Outline

- **Due date:** 2024 was 5pm Friday of the exam period (8 November)
- **Mark weighting:** 2%, marked satisfactory / unsatisfactory
- **Collaboration:** individual
- **Submission:** a Word document via Wattle, using the template

## Description

Earn 1.5--2 hours of research participation credit through
[SONA](https://anupsych.sona-systems.com/) in approved games or HCI
experiments, then write a 100--200 word reflection on what you learned.

:::tip
Start looking for experiments in week 1. Extensions are not granted for
experiment availability.
:::

## Marking

Satisfactory requires at least 1.5 credits from approved experiments *and* a
100--200 word reflection showing evidence of learning.
