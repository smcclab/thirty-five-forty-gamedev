## Purpose

This item asks you to take part in real research in games and human-computer
interaction, and to reflect on what you learned from the experience. Sitting
on the participant side of an experiment is a fast way to understand how
games and interfaces get evaluated -- which is the same skill you use when
you playtest your own work.

## Instructions

During the semester you must earn **1.5 to 2 hours of research participation
credit** (1.5 to 2 credits) through
[SONA](https://anupsych.sona-systems.com/) in games and/or HCI research, and
write a **100 to 200 word** reflection on what you learned.

Your participation must be in an **approved experiment**, as listed on the
Experiment Participation forum on Wattle. If an experiment you want to do is
not on the list, you can request through the forum that it be added. An
experiment is eligible if it is advertised on SONA and is relevant to games
or HCI.

You must take the **course credits** from SONA, not any other form of
remuneration such as payment or a voucher. Participation is checked against
SONA credit records.

:::warning
Start looking for experiments in week 1, so that you can book an experiment
and a time that suit you. Requests for an extension on the grounds of
experiment availability are not granted.
:::

## What the template requires

The template records your UID and name, and has two sections.

**Experiment participation.** A table with one row per experiment: the
experiment ID or name, the date you participated, and the credits earned.

**Reflection (100--200 words).** A brief reflection on your experience
participating in the listed experiments. What did you observe during the
experiment, and what did you learn from the experience? Give specific
examples and reflect on them, rather than describing the procedure.

Submit the completed template as a Word document via Wattle.

## Marking

The item is marked satisfactory or unsatisfactory. Both components must be
satisfactory.

| Component | Satisfactory (2%) | Unsatisfactory (0%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participation | At least 1.5 credits from participating in approved experiments through SONA, taken as course credits rather than any other form of remuneration | Less than 1.5 hours of participation, or participation in experiments that are not approved |
| Reflection | 100--200 words, showing evidence of learning from the experiments | Above or below the word range, or lacking evidence of learning |
