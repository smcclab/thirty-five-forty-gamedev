# COMP3540/6540 --- Game Development

> Course website for COMP3540/COMP6540 Game Development at the ANU School of Computing: lectures, weekly workshops, assessments and resources for a studio course in designing, prototyping and building digital games.

COMP3540/6540 is a course at the ANU School of Computing, Australian National
University, in which students design, prototype and build digital games. It
covers play and the formal elements of games, the playcentric design process,
idea generation, prototyping and playtesting, designing for player engagement,
and producing a game in a team using a game engine.

This site is being rebuilt; dates, template repositories and forum links are
placeholders until the next offering is confirmed. The catalogue entries are
at https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/course/COMP3540 and
https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/course/COMP6540.
